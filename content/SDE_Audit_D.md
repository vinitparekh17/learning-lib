The cross-module lesson holds: permission and feasibility are gates; cost ranking comes afterward. A cheaper model call is not necessarily a cheaper completed case. A faster component is not necessarily a better customer outcome. A reversible internal route is not payment approval. A supplier abstraction does not establish equivalent behavior. These are conclusions within the described contracts, not claims that a real deployment has passed the gates.

The numerical differences between B03 and C04 are intentional. C04 imports the customer inputs and adds 10 U monthly supplier overhead, reducing supplier six-month remainders by 60 U each. C05 changes the permitted job explicitly; C06 then explicitly returns to the earlier evidence job. No hidden cross-run authority expansion was found.
