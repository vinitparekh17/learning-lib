# SDE Foundations — Open Questions Ledger

These questions are intentionally unresolved. They need measurements, stakeholder authority or experience from a real system. Do not fill them with confidence alone.

## Workload and reliability

- What are the offered-arrival, burst, input-length and output-length distributions?
- Which deadlines are component budgets and which are customer promises?
- How often do timeouts leave an unknown external outcome?
- Which failures share a dependency and therefore cannot be multiplied as independent?
- How long must receipts, source revisions and recovery logs remain available?

## Data, evaluation and AI

- What is the actual deployment unit: row, revision, document, customer or organization?
- Which templates, suppliers and revisions connect nominally separate cases?
- Who produces labels, with what disagreement and adjudication process?
- How often does evidence exist but fail at eligibility, retrieval, context selection or support?
- How stable are quality and calibration across segments and time?
- Can the evaluation corpus legally and operationally be reused across suppliers?

## Authority and security

- Which principal is authenticated at every read and effect boundary?
- How fresh must revocation be, and which cached decisions can outlive a policy change?
- Which support, export, repair and recovery paths bypass the normal application route?
- Which effects require two distinct conditions or people?
- What is the contract for historical receipt reads after access changes?

## Capacity and cost

- What are the measured memory peaks, phase curves, startup delay and queue behavior?
- Which costs are variable, committed, tiered, shared or already sunk?
- What labor is released capacity, and what becomes actual cash savings?
- What is the cost per verified timely result, including failures and review?
- Where do capacity tiers or acquisition/setup costs reverse the apparent optimum?

## Customer and product evidence

- Which segment experiences the problem recently and repeatedly?
- Who uses, operates, approves, owns data and controls budget?
- What current workaround is the real competitor?
- What proportion activates and repeats after enough follow-up time?
- Does a bundle solve the complete job or only the visible component?
- Which claimed advantage survives exportability and a credible alternative?

## Personal transfer prompts

- In the insurance POS or claims system, where can a stale rule or revision survive into a financial decision?
- Which report or transaction has an implicit invariant that is checked too early?
- Where does a retry create a new business identity instead of reconciling the first attempt?
- Which metric at work counts only completed cases and hides dropped or delayed work?
- Which AI-generated code review needs evidence about the actual runtime, database or authority boundary?

Add dated answers and evidence beneath each question as you encounter real examples. Keep facts, hypotheses and decisions visibly separate.
